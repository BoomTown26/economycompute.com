Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

How much does the cloud audit cost?

Nothing. The audit is completely free. We earn revenue from data centre partners when we place workloads, not from you.

How much can I save on my cloud bill?

Most companies find savings of 30–60% on non-critical workloads like dev/staging environments, batch jobs, CI/CD pipelines, and AI inference.

What cloud providers do you analyse?

We analyse bills from AWS, Azure, GCP, and other major cloud providers. Upload any cloud bill in CSV or PDF format.

Do I need to change my code to migrate?

No. Your containers and applications run exactly as they are. We handle the migration and placement — your code doesn't change.

How does EconomyCompute make money?

Our partner data centres pay us a commission for placing workloads with them. You pay nothing. Our incentives are fully aligned with saving you money.

Is my billing data secure?

Yes. Your bill is analysed in real-time and never stored. We process the data to generate your report and then discard it. No data retention, no sharing.

What types of workloads can be moved?

Dev/staging environments, batch processing, ML training and inference, CI/CD runners, analytics pipelines, and any workload that doesn't require five-nines uptime. Our AI classifies each workload and only recommends moving what's safe to move.
